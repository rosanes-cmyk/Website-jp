# Maple Sakura School (メイプルサクラスクール), Tsukuba: Research Report

*Prepared 2026-11-19 for a prospective parent or student. Unless marked **Unverified**, everything below comes from the school's own website. That site shows no update dates, so prices and hours may be out of date. Please confirm them directly with the school.*

---

## Overview

- **What it is:** a small English school for children and adults in Gakuen-minami, Tsukuba, Ibaraki ([official site](https://rqkfernandes.wixsite.com/maplesakura)).
- **Founded:** 2013, by "Tom Sensei" ([About page](https://rqkfernandes.wixsite.com/maplesakura/about_us)).
- **Name:** "Maple" comes from the founder's liking for maple trees, and each student is described as a "unique leaf." The site does not explain "Sakura" ([About](https://rqkfernandes.wixsite.com/maplesakura/about_us)).
- **Name variants:** the About page also says "Mapple Sakura School" (a typo) and "Maple Village school / メープルヴィレッジスクール" ([About](https://rqkfernandes.wixsite.com/maplesakura/about_us)).
  - **Unverified:** the school is probably a rebrand of Maple Village School. It appears to have used the same address and phone number, and a former address at 苅間960 / 学園南E57-2 that also offered temporary childcare. No page actually states that the school was renamed. The old domain, maplevillageschool.com, did not resolve when checked on 2026-11-19.
- **Web presence:** the only website found is a free Wix subdomain. Searches found no independent listings, no social media accounts (Instagram, Facebook, LINE, X or YouTube) and no news coverage.
- **Still open?** This is uncertain. The school's email domain (maplesakuraschool.com) reportedly does not resolve (**unverified** in the final check), and the website has no dates on it. That does not prove the school has closed, but call before you visit.

## Fees

Prices are before tax, from the [Programs page](https://rqkfernandes.wixsite.com/maplesakura/Programs):

| Item | Price |
|---|---|
| Kids English Club | ¥10,000 + tax per month (4 lessons) |
| Advanced English Club | ¥10,000 + tax per month (4 lessons) |
| Private tutoring | ¥5,000 + tax per 60-minute session |
| Teacher Delivery | Price on request |
| Materials and use of the space | ¥10,000 + tax. The site does not say whether this is charged once or with every 3-month payment. |
| Trial lesson | Free (30 min) |

**Policies:**
- All fees are paid in advance, 3 months at a time.
- Missed group lessons cannot be rescheduled.
- Leaving requires one month's notice. Without it, one more month may be charged.
- No sibling or multi-course discounts were found.

## Open questions to ask the school

1. Are you still operating, and are the prices and hours on the Wix site current?
2. Is the ¥10,000 materials/space fee charged once, or with every 3-month payment?
3. Do the Advanced and private-lesson hours run from 9 AM to 8 PM?
4. Who teaches the classes: only Tom Sensei, or other teachers too? Is any Japanese used in class?
5. Which textbooks or curriculum do you use? Do you prepare students for Eiken or other exams?
6. Who can join the Advanced English Club (what age and level)?
7. Do you offer sibling or multi-course discounts?
8. How do you handle missed group lessons, given that they cannot be rescheduled?
9. How many parking spaces are there, and how do drop-off and pick-up work?
10. Is Maple Sakura School the former Maple Village School, and when did the name change?
11. Which email address and phone number work best for contacting you?
12. Who operates the school (the business or company name)?
