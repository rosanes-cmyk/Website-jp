Terms

Payment, missed lessons and leaving

Please read these rules before you join. If anything is unclear, just ask us.

Payment
Group lesson fees are paid 3 months in advance (for example, ¥11,000 × 3 months = ¥33,000).
How to pay
Payment methods (cash, bank transfer, etc.) and due dates to be confirmed
Missed lessons
Missed group lessons cannot be rescheduled. Thank you for understanding.
Leaving the school
If you would like to leave, please tell us at least one month in advance. If you tell us later than that, the following month's fee will be charged.
Private tutoring
Charged per lesson (¥5,500, tax incl.). When to pay, and the rules for changing or cancelling a lesson, to be confirmed
