Looking for English classes for your kids in Tsukuba? Maple Sakura School offers four programs: kids classes from age 3 to high school, an advanced class for researchers and working adults, one-to-one private tutoring, and teacher visits to schools and kindergartens. Every program starts with a free 30-minute trial lesson, which also works as a level check.
