FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Tap a question to see the answer. If your question is not here, please feel free to contact us.

Do you offer a free trial lesson?

Yes. We offer a free 30-minute trial lesson, which also serves as a level check. You can get a feel for the classroom and the teacher, and afterwards we will suggest a class that matches your level. To book, use the form on our Access & Contact page or call us at 029-851-0555.

From what age can children join?

The Kids English Club is for children from age 3 up to high school. For adults, we offer the Advanced English Club (academic and business English) and Private Tutoring.

See our programs

How many students are in a class?

Group lessons are small, with 4 to 5 students per class, and each lesson is 50 minutes. Private Tutoring is one-to-one with the teacher, and each session is 60 minutes.

If my child misses a lesson, can we make it up?

Missed group lessons cannot be rescheduled. Thank you for your understanding. We are still confirming the rules for changing or cancelling Private Tutoring sessions, so please ask us.

How do I pay the monthly fee?

For group lessons, we ask you to pay three months in advance (for example, ¥11,000 a month × 3 months = ¥33,000, tax incl.). We are still confirming the payment methods (cash, bank transfer, etc.), so please ask us.

See our payment rules

Is there an enrolment fee?

Yes. There is a one-time enrolment fee of ¥11,000 (tax incl.; ¥10,000 + tax), paid when you join. It covers learning materials and facility costs.

See details on the Fees page

How do I leave the school?

If you wish to leave, please let us know at least one month in advance. If you tell us later than that, the next month's fee will be charged.

Is there parking?

Yes, the school has parking. We are located behind Katsuragi Elementary School. We are still confirming how many cars can park.

See how to get here

Who is the teacher?

Lessons are taught by Tom Sensei, a native English speaker from Canada. He has a master's degree in education and taught English at Japanese elementary and junior high schools for 10 years.

Meet Tom Sensei

Is it OK if I am a complete beginner?

Yes, absolutely. We check your level in the free trial lesson and suggest a class that matches your current English.

Any other questions?

If your question is not answered here, such as Eiken test preparation or online lessons, please feel free to contact us by phone, email or LINE. (Whether we offer Eiken preparation or online lessons is to be confirmed.)
