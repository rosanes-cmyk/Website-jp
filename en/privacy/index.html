Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Maple Sakura School (“we”) takes good care of the personal information that students and parents share with us.

We follow Japan's Act on the Protection of Personal Information and other laws, and handle personal information as follows.

1. What we collect

When you book a trial lesson, contact us or join the school, we ask for:

  • Your name and its kana reading (furigana)
  • Email address and phone number
  • The student's age or school year, and preferred class, days and times
  • Your questions or message

2. Why we use it

  • To arrange trial lessons and enrolment
  • To answer your questions
  • To run lessons (class placement, notices about cancelled lessons and so on)
  • To invoice fees and confirm payments

We will ask for your consent before using it for anything else.

3. Sharing

We never give your personal information to anyone else without your consent, except where the law requires it.

4. Outside services

This website is hosted by Netlify, Inc. (USA). What you enter in the booking form is stored on Netlify's servers, where only the school can see it. Maps are shown with Google Maps and fonts with Google Fonts.

This website does not use cookies for advertising or tracking.

5. Security and how long we keep it

We keep your information safe from loss and leaks. If you take a trial lesson but do not join, we delete your information within X months.

6. Photos of children

We only publish photos showing children with written consent from a parent or guardian. If you want a photo taken down, we will remove it right away.

7. Seeing, correcting or deleting your information

To see, correct, stop the use of, or delete your personal information, please contact us below. Once we have confirmed who you are, we will act promptly.
