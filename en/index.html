Small-group English conversation school in Tsukuba English in Tsukuba: fun, friendly, at your own pace.

Our English conversation school in Tsukuba first opened in 2013 as Maple Village School, and is now starting fresh as Maple Sakura School. A native English teacher from Canada teaches everyone from 3‑year-olds to adults, in small classes of 4–5 students, so each learner gets real attention.
