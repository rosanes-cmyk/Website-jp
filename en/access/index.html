Access & Contact

Getting to Maple Sakura School & How to Contact Us

Here is how to find Maple Sakura School, our English conversation school in Tsukuba. We are in Gakuenminami 3-chome, right behind Katsuragi Elementary School, and there is parking. On this page you will find directions from Kenkyu-gakuen Station (Tsukuba Express), our contact details, and a form to book a free trial lesson.
