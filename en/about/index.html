About

About Maple Sakura School, an English School in Tsukuba

Maple Sakura School is a small English conversation school in Gakuenminami, Tsukuba. We first opened in 2013 as Maple Village School. On this page you can meet our teacher, Tom Sensei, read about how we teach, and see how the school has grown.

Philosophy

How we teach

No two maple leaves are the same shape, and each one grows to its own height. Our students are just the same.

We don't compare anyone with anyone else. Everyone grows their English at their own pace, and in our small classes we teach to where each student is right now.
